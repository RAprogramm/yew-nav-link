## Adding yourself

When your first pull request is merged, open a follow-up PR adding a row
under **Contributors** in the same alphabetical-by-handle order:

```markdown
- **Your Name** ([@your-handle](https://github.com/your-handle)) — short note
  about what you contributed.
```

Or ask the maintainer to do it for you in the PR conversation.
